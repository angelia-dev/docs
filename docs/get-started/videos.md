# Video tour

Two narrated videos, drawn live in your browser on a canvas (no video file to download), with subtitles and
chapters. Press ++space++ to play or pause, ++c++ for subtitles.

<div class="grid" markdown>

[:material-play-circle: **Home Assistant to an agentic PR**<br>The complete recipe: watch, dispatch, tests, review, fix.](../videos/home-assistant/index.html){ .md-button }

</div>

The voice is synthetic ([Kokoro](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), Apache-2.0), generated from a
script kept in this repository; pronunciation was checked by transcribing it back with Whisper.
